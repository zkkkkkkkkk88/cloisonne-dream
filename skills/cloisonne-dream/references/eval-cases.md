# Evaluation Cases

Use these cases to check whether future edits preserve the intended behavior.

## Case 1: Anime character in a garden

Source characteristics:
- single seated character
- parasol
- dense green garden
- wooden railing
- white/lavender clothing

Pass criteria:
- pose and framing remain close to source
- parasol remains present
- garden stays green and structurally similar
- clothing stays white/lavender
- no invented sun, moon, birds, stars, lanterns, or cloud motifs
- photographic texture is replaced by enamel cells and metal partitions

## Case 2: White dragon over fantasy architecture

Source characteristics:
- one large white dragon
- pale castle/city below
- blue daytime sky
- pink-tinted wing membranes

Pass criteria:
- dragon remains the dominant subject
- body pose and wing placement remain recognizable
- city remains below the dragon
- sky remains blue
- wing membranes remain pale pink
- no new celestial symbol or decorative sun disc is invented
- scales and architecture are translated into readable enamel structures

## Case 3: Ice queen with dragon

Source characteristics:
- standing woman in icy dress
- staff
- large dark icy dragon behind her
- dark blue environment

Pass criteria:
- woman, staff, and dragon all remain
- original hierarchy is preserved
- palette stays predominantly cool and dark
- no forced bright-gold background
- no invented moon, stars, snowflake symbols, or decorative birds unless visible in source

## Case 4: Woman on cloud bridge toward bright castle

Source characteristics:
- woman seen in profile/back-three-quarter view
- long pale dress and veil
- winding elevated bridge
- pale castle in distance
- clouds dominate environment
- bright sunlight already exists in source

Pass criteria:
- bridge path, woman placement, castle, clouds, and existing bright sun/light remain recognizable
- the existing sunlight may be restyled because it is actually present
- no additional second sun, moon, birds, emblems, or decorative cloud motifs are introduced

## Failure Triggers

Regenerate or tighten constraints if any of these occur:

- a new sun disc appears where the source had none
- extra birds or stars appear
- background buildings are rearranged or replaced
- character pose changes substantially
- key source colors are replaced by a generic blue-white-gold palette
- the result still looks photographic
- the result resembles stained glass rather than enamel
- gold lines become random ornamental clutter instead of structural partitions
