# Cloisonne Dream

## Overview

Transform the supplied image into a **modern gold-wire cloisonne enamel illustration**.

The core principle is:

**Change the medium, not the scene.**

The output should preserve what the source image contains while replacing photographic, CGI, or painterly rendering with polished enamel color cells, fine metallic partitions, clean handcrafted structure, and restrained glaze highlights.

## Highest-Priority Rule: Content Fidelity

Preserve the source image's:

- subjects and subject count
- identity-defining facial and character features
- animals and creatures
- buildings and objects
- pose and body orientation
- camera viewpoint
- framing and crop intent
- composition
- spatial relationships
- left/right placement
- major silhouettes
- important source colors when identity-relevant

Do not redesign the scene unless the user explicitly asks for redesign.

## No Invented Decorations

Do **not** add an object, symbol, motif, or decorative scene element that is absent from the source merely because it feels suitable for cloisonne, fantasy art, Chinese decorative art, or enamel illustration.

Unless already present in the source or explicitly requested, do not add:

- sun or golden sun disc
- moon
- stars or celestial symbols
- birds
- clouds, stylized clouds, or auspicious cloud motifs
- flowers
- mountains
- extra plants
- lanterns
- gemstones
- snowflakes
- extra buildings
- extra characters or creatures
- ornamental sky symbols
- decorative emblems that change scene meaning

If one of these elements genuinely exists in the source, preserve it and restyle it normally.

## Visual Language

Use a modern cloisonne-inspired enamel look with:

- fine metallic gold or warm-brass partition lines
- clearly separated enamel color regions
- polished opaque or translucent glaze-like surfaces
- subtle dimensional enamel depth
- restrained reflective highlights
- elegant flowing contour rhythm
- simplified but readable detail
- clean handcrafted finish
- rich but controlled color

The final image must no longer look photographic.

## Material Translation

Translate existing content instead of replacing it.

Examples:

- hair strands -> flowing enamel sections divided by fine metal lines
- dragon scales -> individual enamel cells with metallic boundaries
- leaves -> simplified green enamel shapes
- clouds already present -> layered enamel cloud forms
- clothing -> larger enamel fields with metal-line garment structure
- architecture -> geometric enamel panels and metallic structural outlines
- water -> layered blue enamel regions with restrained metallic ripple lines
- skin -> smooth illustrated enamel-toned rendering, not realistic pores

## Detail Strategy

Do not reproduce photographic micro-detail.

Avoid:

- camera noise
- grain
- skin pores
- hyper-real grass blades
- random micro-texture
- fake lens effects
- photographic depth-of-field blur
- photorealistic CGI texture

Replace them with:

- clean color shapes
- enamel cells
- controlled linework
- simplified tonal variation
- glaze-appropriate material texture

## Human and Character Handling

When people or characters are present, preserve:

- identity-defining facial structure
- hairstyle
- pose
- expression
- clothing silhouette
- body orientation
- important costume motifs already visible

Faces should remain attractive and recognizable while clearly rendered as polished illustration/enamel art rather than realistic skin.

Do not redesign the character unless explicitly requested.

## Color Rules

Adapt colors from the source instead of forcing every image into the same palette.

Preferred material families may include:

- ivory white
- jade green
- cobalt blue
- lapis blue
- turquoise
- soft lavender
- coral red
- rose pink
- restrained gold

Gold should mainly serve as metal partition lines and selected accents.

Do not flood the image with gold or turn the entire image into a yellow-gold filter.

## Composition Rules

Follow the source image closely.

Do not:

- move the main subject unnecessarily
- change the camera angle
- crop important subjects
- replace the background
- rearrange buildings
- change left/right relationships
- alter the pose
- introduce a new focal point

Minor simplification is allowed only when needed for enamel readability.

## Style Strength

The final result should clearly read as:

**a handcrafted modern cloisonne enamel illustration**

and not as:

- a photograph with a filter
- photorealistic CGI
- oil painting
- watercolor
- ordinary anime rendering
- stained glass
- flat vector art
- generic fantasy concept art
- 3D render

## Execution Checklist

Before generating or editing the image, verify:

1. Every important source element is accounted for.
2. No new decorative object has been invented.
3. The original pose, composition, viewpoint, and spatial layout remain recognizable.
4. Photographic micro-texture is being replaced rather than reproduced.
5. Gold lines function as enamel partitions rather than decorative clutter.
6. Source colors are adapted, not arbitrarily recolored.
7. The result clearly belongs to the same Cloisonne Dream visual family.

If a newly invented decorative element appears, remove it and regenerate with stronger source-fidelity constraints.
